The idea项目思路

Universal Manipulation Interface (UMI) showed that a hand-held gripper with a wrist camera lets people collect robot demonstrations anywhere, far faster than teleoperation. But a wrist camera cannot tell how hard the fingers are squeezing, whether an object has started to slip, or what a cloth feels like. We added touch: two GelSight fingertips record a tactile video stream next to the RGB stream, Quest tracking supplies the 6-DoF pose, and an ArUco marker gives the gripper width.

Universal Manipulation Interface（UMI）证明了带手腕相机的手持夹爪可以让人在任何地方采集机器人示教，比遥操作快得多。但手腕相机看不出手指捏得多紧、物体是否开始打滑、一块布摸起来是什么感觉。于是我们加上了触觉：两个 GelSight 指尖在 RGB 流旁边同步记录触觉视频，Quest 追踪提供六自由度位姿，ArUco 标记给出夹爪开度。

The hard part is not the sensors but trusting them together. Four streams with four clocks and four coordinate frames have to become one robot-ready timeline, and every transformation has to be measured before it is used. We treated calibration and data quality as part of the learning system, not as hidden setup—every episode is inspectable from acquisition through alignment, curation and deployment.

难点不在传感器本身，而在于让它们一起可信。四路数据流、四个时钟、四个坐标系必须合成一条机器人可用的时间线，每一个变换在使用前都要先测出来。我们把标定和数据质量当作学习系统的一部分，而不是看不见的前置工作——每条示教从采集、对齐、筛选到部署都可以被检视。

Then the corpus split into two policy branches. My lab mate trained a tactile-conditioned Diffusion Policy; I converted the same demonstrations to LeRobot format and fine-tuned π0.5, a vision-language-action model, and deployed it on the FR3. This page covers the π0.5 branch.

随后数据被用于两条策略分支：实验室同学训练了触觉条件化的 Diffusion Policy；我把同一批示教转换成 LeRobot 格式，微调视觉-语言-动作模型 π0.5，并部署到 FR3 上。这一页介绍 π0.5 这一分支。

What is calibrated, and how it is reused标定了什么，以及如何复用

Four things are calibrated: camera intrinsics, the Quest-to-camera hand–eye geometry, the robot-side camera and tool relations (FR3 eye-in-hand), and gripper width. The hand–eye solution follows the Park–Martin closed form over 40 poses; the notation is A_T_B for the transform that maps B coordinates into A, so the demonstration chain is W_T_C = W_T_Q · Q_T_C and the robot execution chain is B_T_F · F_T_C and F_T_TCP. The Quest/Unity axis conversion is treated as a basis change, not as part of the extrinsic.

标定的内容有四项：相机内参、Quest 到相机的手眼几何、机器人侧的相机与工具关系（FR3 眼在手上）、以及夹爪开度。手眼求解采用 Park–Martin 闭式解，使用 40 组位姿；记号 A_T_B 表示把 B 坐标映射到 A 的变换，示教链路为 W_T_C = W_T_Q · Q_T_C，机器人执行链路为 B_T_F · F_T_C 与 F_T_TCP。Quest/Unity 的坐标轴转换被当作基变换处理，而不是外参的一部分。

Reuse is explicit: whenever hardware changed between tasks we recalibrated, while the unchanged insertion set-up reused its calibration across both collection days (19 and 23 June), with serialised matrices agreeing to within 10⁻⁷. Gripper width goes through two lookup tables—a five-point UMI table from pixel separation of the finger markers to physical gap, then a Robotiq table from gap to driver position—so the same width means the same thing on the hand-held device and on the robot.

复用是显式的：任务之间硬件一旦变化就重新标定；而插入任务的装置没有变化，两次采集日（6 月 19 日与 23 日）复用同一套标定，序列化矩阵的差异在 10⁻⁷ 以内。夹爪开度经过两张查找表——先用五点 UMI 表把手指标记的像素间距换算为物理开口，再用 Robotiq 表把开口换算为驱动器位置——这样同一个开度在手持设备和机器人上的含义完全一致。

Aligning two clocks with one motion用一次运动对齐两个时钟

With the camera and the Quest rigidly mounted on the UMI, the device moves in front of a stationary ArUco marker. The camera estimates the marker’s position from video and calibrated intrinsics; the Quest records the controller trajectory, transformed into the camera frame by the measured hand–eye transform. Following the one-axis alignment of exUMI, we compare normalised velocity along a matching axis and search for the scalar time offset that minimises the mean-squared error between the two signals.

相机与 Quest 刚性固定在 UMI 上，设备在一个静止的 ArUco 标记前运动。相机根据视频和标定内参估计标记位置；Quest 记录手柄轨迹，并通过测得的手眼变换转换到相机坐标系。参照 exUMI 的单轴对齐方法，我们比较同一轴上的归一化速度，搜索使两路信号均方误差最小的标量时间偏移。

The accepted run estimated −56.738 ms: the main camera trails the Quest by about 56.7 ms, so Quest timestamps are shifted later to align the streams. The fit used 437 ArUco detections against 1,814 Quest samples with one-sample Gaussian smoothing, and the displayed mean-squared error fell from 0.523 to 0.476 after the shift. This is a measured value for this set-up, not a universal hardware latency—and that distinction is exactly why the ledger exists.

被接受的那次运行估计出 −56.738 ms：主相机比 Quest 晚约 56.7 ms，因此把 Quest 时间戳后移以对齐数据流。拟合使用了 437 次 ArUco 检测与 1,814 个 Quest 采样，做单样本高斯平滑，平移后显示的均方误差从 0.523 降到 0.476。这是针对本套装置的实测值，而非通用的硬件时延——这个区别正是账本存在的意义。

Tactile cameras: a shared flicker instead of motion触觉相机：用共同的闪烁而不是运动

The tactile cameras cannot see the ArUco marker, so their offset is measured differently: both the main camera and a tactile camera observe the same light flicker, and the cross-correlation of their brightness traces gives the offset independently of any Quest motion. The recorded offsets are 0 ms and +25 ms, assigned per session and sensor slot in the metadata.

触觉相机看不到 ArUco 标记，所以它们的偏移用另一种方式测量：主相机和触觉相机同时观察同一个灯光闪烁，两条亮度曲线的互相关直接给出偏移，与 Quest 运动无关。记录到的偏移为 0 ms 与 +25 ms，按采集会话和传感器槽位写入元数据。

Causal observation windows因果观测窗口

Alignment is not only about matching timestamps; at run time a policy may only use what had actually arrived when it decided. We model each stream with its assumed latency—wrist RGB about 55 ms, Quest 10 ms, left tactile 35 ms, right tactile 90 ms—and for a decision at +80 ms with a ±60 ms matching tolerance the right tactile stream contributes its closest available frame (−45 ms), not the newest one. Mapped-time ordering and decision-time availability are checked separately, and an action-chunk index of 1 was chosen empirically as the lookahead in robot trials.

对齐不只是时间戳匹配；运行时策略只能使用在做决策那一刻已经到达的数据。我们给每一路流一个假定时延——手腕 RGB 约 55 ms、Quest 10 ms、左触觉 35 ms、右触觉 90 ms——在 +80 ms 做决策、匹配容差 ±60 ms 的例子里，右触觉流贡献的是最近可用的那一帧（−45 ms），而不是最新到达的那一帧。时间映射后的顺序与决策时刻的可用性是两项独立检查；机器人试验中按经验选择动作块索引 1 作为前瞻。

Record first, align second, audit before training先记录，再对齐，训练前先审计

Raw streams keep their native timestamps in H5; alignment and camera-relative pose derivation are reproducible post-processing steps. A command-line audit checks stream completeness, timing faults and inverse-kinematics plausibility; a web audit lets us replay any episode with all streams side by side. Only accepted trajectories are promoted to the clean Zarr dataset that training consumes.

原始流在 H5 中保留各自的时间戳；对齐与相机相对位姿的推导是可重复的后处理步骤。命令行审计检查流完整性、时序故障与逆运动学可行性；网页审计则可以把任意一条示教的所有流并排回放。只有通过审计的轨迹才会被提升到训练所用的干净 Zarr 数据集。

What the timing checks found时序检查的结果

A read-only pass over the 223 raw episodes found no duplicate or backward host timestamps; the export holds 222 episodes and 135,952 policy steps, and no tactile window reaches outside its episode. Because tactile cameras run slower than the policy step, six-frame tactile windows reuse source frames 15.9% of the time on the left finger and 38.2% on the right, and the latest frame in a window can be up to 90.2 ms (left) or 178.2 ms (right) old. These numbers describe the data as it is; the receive timestamps do not record preprocessing completion or policy decision deadlines, which is a stated limitation.

对 223 条原始示教的只读检查没有发现重复或倒退的主机时间戳；导出结果包含 222 条示教、135,952 个策略步，没有任何触觉窗口越出所属示教。由于触觉相机帧率低于策略步长，六帧触觉窗口在左指有 15.9%、右指有 38.2% 的时间复用了源帧，窗口中最新一帧的映射年龄最大分别为 90.2 ms（左）和 178.2 ms（右）。这些数字描述的是数据本身的状态；接收时间戳并不记录预处理完成时刻或策略决策截止时刻，这是明确写出的局限。

Three representations of one episode同一条示教的三种表示

Every episode exists in three linked forms: raw H5 with native timestamps, aligned H5 after offset correction and pose derivation, and the training Zarr. The Zarr stores absolute future camera targets (one step ahead); the policy loader turns them into relative SE(3) actions at load time, keeps gripper width absolute, and future targets are used only as supervision, never as inputs. The catalog of these representations—475 logical episodes including a DROID LeRobot export—can be browsed in RobotData Studio, an independent local-first data workspace that Suhang Xia built around this corpus.

每条示教都以三种相互关联的形式存在：保留原生时间戳的原始 H5、偏移校正和位姿推导后的对齐 H5、以及训练用的 Zarr。Zarr 存的是绝对的未来相机目标（向前一步）；策略加载器在加载时把它们转换为相对的 SE(3) 动作，夹爪开度保持绝对值，未来目标只作监督、不作输入。这些表示的目录——475 条逻辑示教，含一份 DROID LeRobot 导出——可以在 RobotData Studio 中浏览，那是夏苏杭围绕这套数据构建的本地优先的独立数据工作台。

Fine-tuning π0.5 on the corpus在语料上微调 π0.5

π0.5 is a vision-language-action model: a PaliGemma vision-language backbone with an action expert that generates action chunks by flow matching, pre-trained on large robot corpora and designed to be fine-tuned on a few hundred demonstrations. That makes it a natural match for a UMI corpus, which is exactly a few hundred demonstrations with a wrist view and a 7-D action.

π0.5 是一个视觉-语言-动作模型：PaliGemma 视觉语言骨干加上一个用流匹配生成动作块的动作专家，在大规模机器人数据上预训练，设计目标就是用几百条示教微调。这与 UMI 语料天然匹配——它正是几百条带手腕视角和 7 维动作的示教。

My branch: convert the clean Zarr corpus into LeRobot format (images, proprioceptive state, action chunks and a natural-language task prompt per episode), compute the normalisation statistics, fine-tune π0.5 with LoRA on a single GPU using the openpi stack, and serve the policy to the FR3 over a websocket inference server. At run time the policy receives the wrist image and robot state, predicts a 50-step chunk of camera-relative 7-D actions, and the controller converts them through inverse kinematics into joint commands. Rollouts were scored live with a success counter.

我的分支：把干净的 Zarr 语料转换成 LeRobot 格式（每条示教包含图像、本体状态、动作块和一条自然语言任务指令），计算归一化统计量，用 openpi 在单卡上以 LoRA 微调 π0.5，再通过 websocket 推理服务器把策略提供给 FR3。运行时策略接收手腕图像与机器人状态，预测 50 步相机相对的 7 维动作块，控制器经逆运动学转换为关节指令。实机回放用成功计数器实时记录。

On the robot, both branches share one execution chain: a TCP bridge maps camera-relative targets into FR3 and Robotiq commands, and Polymetis runs impedance control on the arm, so the FR3 carries the same Robotiq gripper and GelSight fingertips the hand-held device had.

在机器人上两条分支共用同一条执行链路：一个 TCP 桥把相机相对目标映射为 FR3 与 Robotiq 指令，Polymetis 在机械臂上运行阻抗控制；FR3 装着与手持设备相同的 Robotiq 夹爪和 GelSight 指尖。

The parallel branch: UniForce + Diffusion Policy并行分支：UniForce + Diffusion Policy

The lab mate’s branch asks a narrower question on the same corpus: does a force-aware tactile representation help a Diffusion Policy? UniForce is trained in two stages—pre-trained on Taichi-simulated marker-field deformation under varied indenters and loading trajectories, then fine-tuned on paired, frame-aligned presses from the custom GelSight and the GelSight Mini (six indenters, 1,000 frames each)—and frozen before policy training. Six causal frames per fingertip become eight contact tokens that condition the policy alongside wrist RGB and state through a frozen DINO ViT-B with LoRA in its last four blocks; the policy predicts an eight-step chunk of camera-relative 7-D actions.

同学的分支在同一套语料上问一个更窄的问题：力感知的触觉表示能否帮助 Diffusion Policy？UniForce 分两阶段训练——先在 Taichi 仿真的标记点形变（不同压头与加载轨迹）上预训练，再用自制 GelSight 与 GelSight Mini 上配对、帧对齐的真实按压（六种压头，各 1,000 帧）微调——训练策略前冻结。每个指尖六帧因果历史变成八个接触 token，与手腕 RGB 和状态一起（经最后四层带 LoRA 的冻结 DINO ViT-B）条件化策略；策略预测八步相机相对的 7 维动作块。

In a controlled evaluation of 68 physical rollouts on the 77-demonstration strict subset (65 training, 12 validation; task: move a white cylinder to a white plate, success = contact), vision-only Diffusion Policy reached 10/34 (29.4%, Wilson 95% CI 16.8–46.2%) and UniForce + Diffusion Policy 14/34 (41.2%, CI 26.4–57.8%)—a +11.8-point difference that a two-sided Fisher exact test does not call significant (p = 0.447). The π0.5 branch is kept separate from that comparison; its contribution is showing that the same calibrated pipeline yields trajectories a modern VLA can learn from and execute on the real FR3.
