About关于我

I am Chengqi Xue, an MSc Robotics student at King’s College London (2025–2026). My undergraduate degree is in Automation from Yangtze University, where I graduated in the top 7% with a National Scholarship. My research is in embodied intelligence and multimodal robot learning: how vision, touch and language can be combined so that a robot acts well in contact-rich tasks.

我叫薛程琪，伦敦国王学院机器人工程硕士（2025–2026），本科就读于长江大学自动化专业，专业排名前 7%，获国家奖学金。研究方向是具身智能与多模态机器人学习：如何把视觉、触觉和语言结合起来，让机器人在富接触任务中做出可靠的动作。

I have the full path from algorithm to real hardware: on a Franka arm I built an end-to-end vision–tactile–language–action system with π0.5 as the VLA backbone, Qwen2.5-VL for reasoning and GelSight for touch, and finished with a real-robot demonstration. Before that, four years as a research assistant taught me to turn industrial problems—unlit thread bores, 72-sample datasets, hazardous well sites—into published, patented systems.

我有从算法到真机落地的完整经验：在 Franka 机械臂上以 π0.5 作为 VLA 骨干，结合 Qwen2.5-VL 和 GelSight 触觉感知，搭建了端到端的视觉-触觉-语言-动作系统，并完成真机演示。此前四年科研助理经历，让我习惯把工业现场的问题——无光的螺纹孔、只有 72 组数据的试验、危险的井场——变成发表的论文、授权的专利和能运行的系统。

How I work: break the problem apart first, then let data settle the argument. I can drive a project on my own and I like working across teams; English is a working language for me. What I want next is to put embodied-intelligence algorithms into real product settings.

做事习惯先把问题拆清楚，再用数据验证结论；能独立推进项目，也愿意和不同团队协作；英语可以作为工作语言。接下来希望能在真实的产品场景里把具身智能算法落地。

Vision–Tactile–Language–Action learning for robotic fabric sorting视觉-触觉-语言-动作（VTLA）学习用于机器人织物分拣

Can a robot that cannot see the difference between two garments feel it, explain it, and act on it?

两件看起来几乎一样的衣物，机器人能否靠“摸”分辨出来，再用语言说清楚，并据此完成分拣？

An end-to-end system on a Franka arm: a GelSight fingertip presses hanging garments, a ResNet-18 load estimator replaces the force sensor at run time (MAE 0.33 N on unseen fabrics), a QLoRA-tuned Qwen2.5-VL compares softness, thickness, elasticity and texture, and the arm ranks four garments, picks one and places it in a basket. π0.5 served as the VLA backbone explored for the action policy.

在 Franka 机械臂上搭建的端到端系统：GelSight 指尖按压悬挂衣物，ResNet-18 力估计器在部署阶段取代力传感器（未见织物 MAE 0.33 N），QLoRA 微调的 Qwen2.5-VL 比较柔软度、厚度、弹性与纹理，机械臂对四件衣物排序、挑选并放入篮筐。动作策略以 π0.5 作为 VLA 骨干进行探索。

Tactile UMI: from hand-held visuotactile demonstrations to a π0.5 policy on a Franka FR3Tactile UMI：从手持视触觉示教到 Franka FR3 上的 π0.5 策略

How do hand-held demonstrations with touch become calibrated, auditable data a VLA policy can actually learn from?

带触觉的手持示教，如何变成标定可靠、可审计、能真正训练 VLA 策略的数据？

With a lab mate I built a UMI-style hand-held gripper with wrist RGB, dual GelSight fingertips, Quest motion tracking and gripper state; calibrated geometry and clocks (hand–eye, 56.7 ms Quest–camera offset, gripper-width lookup) and audited every episode. He trained a Diffusion Policy on the corpus; I fine-tuned π0.5 on the same demonstrations and ran it on the FR3.

我和实验室同学一起搭建了带手腕相机、双 GelSight 指尖、Quest 运动追踪与夹爪状态的 UMI 式手持夹爪；完成几何与时钟标定（手眼标定、56.7 ms Quest–相机时延、夹爪开度查表），并对每条示教做审计。他用这批数据训练 Diffusion Policy，我则在同一批示教上微调 π0.5，并在 FR3 上实机运行。

TIAGo assistive robot for chemotherapy wards医院辅助患者的 TIAGo 自主服务机器人

Can a ward robot notice a patient who needs help, reach them safely, and stay out of the way of clinical staff?

病房里的机器人能否察觉需要帮助的患者、安全地走到他们身边，又不打扰医护工作？

A ROS 2 + Webots system for the TIAGo platform: speech trigger (Faster-Whisper) followed by MediaPipe wave detection, RGB-D target localisation, and a full SLAM → AMCL → Nav2 pipeline that stops at a social stand-off distance. 92% end-to-end success and 94% navigation success over simulated ward trials.

基于 TIAGo 平台的 ROS 2 + Webots 系统：语音触发（Faster-Whisper）后由 MediaPipe 检测挥手，RGB-D 定位患者，再经 SLAM → AMCL → Nav2 完整导航流水线，在社交安全距离处停下。病房仿真试验端到端成功率 92%，导航成功率 94%。

Multi-vision and deep-learning inspection of internal threads基于多视觉与深度学习的内螺纹缺陷检测系统

How do you inspect a thread that lives inside a dark, curved, 100 mm bore, when defect samples are too rare to train on?

螺纹藏在黑暗、弯曲的 100 mm 孔内，缺陷样本又极其稀少，如何做到自动检测？

A six-fisheye ring probe with strip lighting, cylindrical-model panorama stitching where planar methods fail, DFMGAN few-shot defect synthesis, and YOLOv8 detection (mAP 91.6%). A robot-arm carrier cut acquisition time by 44%.

六目鱼眼环形探头配条形光源；在平面拼接全部失效的曲面场景下提出柱面投影全景拼接；用 DFMGAN 少样本生成缺陷图像；YOLOv8 检测 mAP 91.6%。机械臂采集方案比步进电机方案提速 44%。

Small-sample machine learning for engineering-structure modelling and optimisation小样本机器学习驱动的工程结构性能建模与优化

With only 72 destructive tests, can a model predict how a composite column will fail—and tell you how to design a better one?

只有 72 组破坏性试验数据，模型能否预测复合材料柱的承载性能，并反过来指导设计？

Genetic-algorithm data augmentation (72 → 15,000 virtual samples), a Reptile meta-learning regressor reaching R² 0.982 for ultimate bearing capacity and 0.955 for displacement, and a TPOT AutoML benchmark that yields concrete optimal section designs.

遗传式数据增广（72 → 15,000 组虚拟样本）；基于 Reptile 的元学习回归模型，极限承载力 R² 0.982、极限位移 R² 0.955；TPOT AutoML 对比实验并反演出最优截面设计方案。

Machine-vision monitoring and early warning for hazardous well sites基于机器视觉的工业监测预警系统

On an open well site full of flammable gas, how do you catch a missing hard hat—and tell workers which way to run?

在易燃易爆气体环境的井场上，如何自动发现没戴安全帽的工人，并在泄漏时告诉大家往哪跑？

A three-axis (rotate / extend / pitch) camera gantry that sweeps the wellhead and flags missing hard hats and smoking on an embedded box, plus a solar-powered beacon that couples an H₂S detector with a wind vane so an LED arrow always points to the up-wind escape route.

旋转/伸缩/俯仰三自由度视觉巡检机构，在嵌入式控制盒上识别未戴安全帽与吸烟行为；以及光伏供电的预警装置，把硫化氢检测与风向标联动，LED 箭头始终指向逆风逃生方向。

Embedded vision · stepper/servo mechanisms · MCU · H₂S sensing · solar power · patent drafting

Dual-stream action recognition for industrial human–robot collaboration (HRI30)面向工业人机协作的双流动作识别（HRI30）

Can RGB appearance and skeleton motion be fused so a robot reads what a worker is doing?

把 RGB 外观与骨架运动融合，机器人能否读懂工人正在做什么？

Two branches on the HRI30 dataset (30 industrial actions): a Kinetics-pretrained 3D ResNet-18 on 16-frame clips and a six-block ST-GCN on YOLOv8-Pose skeletons, fused by weighted late fusion. Built end to end—preprocessing, training, batched inference and submission tooling.

在 HRI30 数据集（30 类工业动作）上搭建双分支：Kinetics 预训练的 3D ResNet-18 处理 16 帧片段，六层 ST-GCN 处理 YOLOv8-Pose 骨架序列，加权后期融合。从预处理、训练到批量推理与结果导出全链路独立完成。

PyTorch · torchvision r3d_18 · YOLOv8-Pose · ST-GCN · OpenCV · late fusion
